# Extra Armory
A Minecraft 1.7.10 mod add-on for [Balkon's WeaponMod: Legacy](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/balkons-weaponmod-legacy)

That adds new weapons for modded materials.

> Yes, even their special effects.

# Currently Includes
## Generic
* Copper
* Tin
## [Twilight Forest](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-twilight-forest)
* Ironwood
* Steeleaf
* Knightmetal
* Fiery
## [Aether](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/aether)
* Skyroot
* Holystone
* Zanite
* Gravitite
## [Aether Legacy Departure](https://github.com/AetherLegacyPro/AetherLegacyDeparture)
* Arkenium
* Continuum
## [Biomes O' Plenty](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biomes-o-plenty)
* Ender Amethyst
## [Erebus](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-erebus)
