# Extra Armory

An add-on for [Balkon's WeaponMod: Legacy!](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/balkons-weaponmod-legacy)

![All the weapons](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2115/682/list.png "All Weapons")

Adds support for the following materials, including their on-hit special effects and configurable, brand new effects for some weapons as well.

## **Effects**
_Also applies to flail, spear, knife and boomerang projectiles as well!_

### **Twilight Forest**
*   Ironwood and Steeleaf weapons are pre-enchanted
*   Fiery weapons set targets on fire
*   Knightmetal weapons deal extra damage to **armored targets** with the exception being the boomerang, warhammer, flail and halberd that deal extra damage to **unarmored targets** instead.
*   Knightmetal Halberd can switch to dealing damage to armored or unarmored targets depending on the halberd state (On Right-Click)

### **Aether**
*   Skyroot weapons have their double drops
*   Zanite weapons get stronger the lower their durability is
*   Gravitite weapons fling the target into the air

### **Aether Legacy Departure**
*   Arkenium weapons have +1 range
*   Gravitite weapons fling the target slightly less high into the air with particles to mirror the base Gravitite sword from this version
*   All Aether weapons have matching tooltips exclusive to Legacy Departure

### **Netherlicious**
*   Efrine weapons are fireproof and don't burn in lava

## **New Special Effects**  
Some materials have new effects to justify using them since I felt like they didn't really do much for how tedious or rare they are to obtain or craft.

### **Jade (Erebus)**
*   Deals extra damage to arthropods. Different from Bane of Arthropods as this effect is built-in
*   Also applies to the base Jade Sword from Erebus
*   Can be disabled in the configs

### **Neptunium (Aquaculture)**
*   Deals extra damage to mobs in/under water, in the rain or if the mob is an aquatic mob. Basically Impaling from the modern versions
*   Also applies to the base Neptunium weapons and tools from Aquaculture
*   Can be disabled in the configs

### **Ender Amethyst (Biomes O' Plenty)**
*   Deals damage to all mobs in an area
*   Does not hurt tamed mobs
*   Effect is disabled when sneaking
*   Also applies to the base Amethyst Sword from Biomes O' Plenty
*   Can be disabled in the configs

## **Loot Integration**
Currently only Neptunium weapons are integrated.  
Neptunium Weapons can be obtained by opening Neptune's Bounty just like rest of Neptunium gear  
Can be disabled in the configs
